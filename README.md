# E-Commerce Sales Analysis (PowerBI Dashboard))

## Purpose
Visualizes user purchase behaviors, spending patterns, and regional sales to turn raw transactional data into actionable business insights.
Utilizing  foundational skills of data analysis within the tool.

## Key Insights Visualized
* **Profitability:** Tracks monthly net profit trends to identify peak seasons and margin drops.
* **Product Performance:** Highlights top-performing sub-categories (Printers, Bookcases, Sarees).
* **Category Share:** Displays volume distribution across Clothing (63%), Electronics (21%), and Furniture (17%).
* **Geography:** Maps sales performance across key states (Maharashtra, MP, UP, Delhi).
* **Payment Modes:** Breaks down transaction types, led by Cash on Delivery (COD) and UPI.

## Data Interpretations & Business Takeaways
* **Seasonal Demand Shifts:** Profit peaks sharply in November and January, likely driven by holiday/festival shopping, while May shows the steepest net loss, signaling a need for targeted off-season promotions.
* **Inventory Prioritization:** Clothing commands the overwhelming majority of order volume at 63%, meaning the business should prioritize supply chain reliability for apparel.
* **High-Value Drivers:** While Clothing drives volume, sub-categories like Printers and Bookcases generate the highest net profit margins, proving that low-volume tech/furniture items are critical for the bottom line.
* **Payment Ecosystem:** Cash on Delivery (COD) remains the dominant payment preference at 44%, followed by UPI at 21%, indicating that checkout optimization should focus heavily on smoothing out the COD and mobile-wallet pipelines.
